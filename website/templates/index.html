CensorLab lets circumvention developers test their tools against the censor strategies of the past, present, and future. By proactively censoring their own protocols, developers can build stronger threat models and harden their tools before a real censor finds the weakness.

What you can emulate

Layered filtering

Packets flow through Ethernet, ARP, IPv4/IPv6, ICMP, TCP and UDP layers, each with its own allow and block lists. IP lists take CIDR subnets.

Programmable censors

Write per-connection logic in Python, or in CensorLang, a small DSL that is type- and bounds-checked when it loads.

ML classifiers

Load ONNX models and call them from your censor to emulate data-driven traffic classification that no censor has deployed yet.

Protocol-aware

Strict parsers for DNS, TLS ClientHellos reassembled across segments and records, and decrypted QUIC Initials (v1 and v2).

Real censor actions

Drop, inject TCP RSTs, forge DNS responses, delay, rewrite TTLs, or buffer IPv4 fragments the way TSPU middleboxes do.

Three deployment modes

Intercept live traffic with netfilter queues, run inline as a transparent bridge between two interfaces, or replay a PCAP offline.
